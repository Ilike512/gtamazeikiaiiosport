# Sound effects

- The base vehicle horn comes from [Car signal](https://opengameart.org/content/car-signal), published under CC0. The sedan, van and patrol cues are derived from that waveform.
- The patrol siren uses [Police lezer's public-domain recording](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:American_police_siren_i.ogg).
- The wanted cue, defeat cue, rain, and water splash were synthesized for the project. Their source is in `tools/generate_game_cues.mjs`.
- `menu_hover.mp3`, `menu_click.mp3` and `character_switch.mp3` were supplied for the project. They are replaceable from the Custom Assets folder.
