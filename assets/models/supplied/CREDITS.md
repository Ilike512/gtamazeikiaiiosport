# Supplied models

The project uses the original meshes supplied in the seven archives. Godot imports the FBX or GLB files. The game adjusts their scale and placement and provides collision for the Backrooms and larger props.

- Blitzo Character Design by t.flores. The listing specifies [Creative Commons Attribution](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The prototype restores the supplied head and clothing textures, adds a movement rig, and adjusts scale, orientation and the tail for gameplay. [Source](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/blitzo-character-design-f8bc7182497f44b687e1ae667c050bec)
