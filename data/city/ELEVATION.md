# Mažeikiai elevation

`elevation.json` was produced using Copernicus WorldDEM-30 © DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018 provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA; all rights reserved.

Source tile `Copernicus_DSM_COG_10_N56_00_E022_00_DEM`. [Copernicus DEM data and license](https://doi.org/10.5270/ESA-c5d3d65)

The derivative grid covers the Mažeikiai-area map on a 23 metre game grid. It is resampled from the source elevation, lightly filtered to reduce building and vegetation spikes, and stored relative to a 69.9 m height datum. Grid heights run from −26.90 m to +32.43 m relative to that datum. The hills and river valley follow measured regional relief. Street surfaces and building foundations are fitted to that grid, so their fine grades are approximate.
