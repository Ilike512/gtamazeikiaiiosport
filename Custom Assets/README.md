# Drop-in assets

Drop `menu.png` or `logo.png` here to change the title screen. Replace loading art in `loading/`, music and vehicle sounds in `sound/`, and radio art in `station_icons/`. See the [project README](../README.md) for exact filenames and supported formats.

Drop `studio_profile.png` here to replace the splash portrait. Use `sound/studio_start.mp3` for the studio splash and `sound/loading.mp3` for the loop that plays after **PLAY** or **CONTINUE**.
